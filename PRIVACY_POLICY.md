# Select AI Privacy Policy

> Repository copy of the English privacy policy published at [select-ai.cn/privacy](https://www.select-ai.cn/privacy). For the Chinese version, see [PRIVACY_POLICY.zh-CN.md](PRIVACY_POLICY.zh-CN.md).

## Privacy Policy

Learn how we protect your privacy

### Data Collection and Processing

- User Input: When you select text on a webpage, the extension sends it to AI services for processing.
- Built-in AI: When using the free built-in AI service, your selected text is sent to our backend server for AI processing.
- Custom AI: You can also configure your own API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, MiniMax, Zhipu AI, etc.). When using a custom AI provider, text is sent directly to that provider.
- API Keys: User-provided API keys are stored locally in your browser and are never transmitted to our servers.

### Data Storage

- Local Storage: API keys and user preferences are stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local.
- Account Data: When you create an account, we collect your username, email address, and password (hashed) for authentication purposes only.
- Usage Records: We store usage logs including the AI model used, input/output character count, request latency, and status for service management and quota tracking.
- Feedback: When you submit feedback or suggestions, we may collect your contact information for follow-up purposes.

### Third-Party Disclosure

When using the built-in free AI service, text is processed through our backend service. When using a custom AI provider, text is sent directly to the provider you configured. Please refer to the respective AI provider's privacy policy for their data handling practices.

### Children's Privacy

This extension is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document.

### Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the extension's support channel.
